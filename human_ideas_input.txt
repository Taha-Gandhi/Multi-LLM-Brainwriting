Idea 1:
Title: Anonymous Brainwriting Board
Description: A web platform where participants submit ideas anonymously, discuss each idea in threads, and use a general chat to converge.

Idea 2:
Title: Thread-Based Voting
Description: Each idea has a discussion thread, and people vote only after reading the full discussion, critique, and improvement history.

Idea 3:
Title: Anonymous Group Chat
Description: A general chat lets participants compare ideas overall and negotiate toward one final collaborative idea.
