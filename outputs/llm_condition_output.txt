================================================================================

DESIGN CHALLENGE
Design a solution that improves creative collaboration and idea selection in group brainwriting.

STAGE 1: ORIGINAL LLM IDEAS
================================================================================

GPT_Agent:
Idea Title: Sync‑Pulse Brainwriting Board  
Problem: Traditional brainwriting piles ideas on paper, then groups waste time reading, voting, and reconciling conflicting thoughts, leading to dominance bias and lost nuance.  
Why It Is Useful: A shared digital canvas shows each participant’s contributions in real‑time, tags them with a pulse‑rating (1‑5) that auto‑aggregates into a heat map, letting the group instantly spot high‑energy concepts while preserving every voice; the heat map guides a quick, data‑driven shortlist without lengthy debates.

Creative_Agent:
Idea Title: “Echo Bubble” Problem: In group brainwriting, ideas get lost in the noise and lack structured comparison. Why It Is Useful: Echo Bubble creates a virtual, time‑stamped bubble for each idea that automatically gathers related micro‑ideas, scores them via peer reaction sliders, and highlights emerging themes, so teams can instantly see which concepts resonate, refine them in real time, and cherry‑pick the most vibrant ideas for deeper play.

Practical_Agent:
Idea Title: Silent Voting Stacks

Problem: In brainwriting, dominant voices still steer selection, and anonymous written ideas get lost in group discussion.

Why It Is Useful: After silent writing, participants stack all idea cards face-down, then each person silently removes one card they think is weakest—no discussion, just quick removal until 5-7 survive. This surfaces quality without groupthink bias.

STAGE 3: MERGED CANDIDATE IDEAS
================================================================================

Candidate 1:
Title: Pulse‑Bubble Canvas
Concept: A digital whiteboard where each written idea appears as a bubble that users can rate (1‑5). Ratings generate a live heat‑map overlay; bubbles automatically cluster by shared keywords, forming thematic zones. After the heat‑map highlights “hot” clusters, participants drag low‑pulse bubbles into a discard bin for a quick final trim.
Merged Elements: Sync‑Pulse real‑time heat‑map, Echo Bubble automatic clustering/tagging, gentle drag‑to‑discard trim.

Candidate 2:
Title: Silent‑Tag Stack System
Concept: Paper‑based brainwriting where each card includes a small keyword tag. After silent writing, cards are stacked; participants silently move cards into themed piles based on tags. A quick “thumbs‑up” pass lets each person place a token on their favorite cards; the top‑rated cards in each pile become the shortlist.
Merged Elements: Practical Silent Voting low‑tech flow, Echo Bubble keyword tagging for theme detection, light pulse‑style thumbs‑up for data‑driven refinement.

Candidate 3:
Title: Hybrid Pulse‑Trim Workshop
Concept: Teams use a simple digital app (or spreadsheet) to log ideas with a 1‑5 pulse rating. The app auto‑generates a heat‑map and groups ideas by AI‑suggested keywords. After the heat‑map reveals top clusters, a brief silent removal round eliminates the lowest‑rated ideas, leaving 5‑7 high‑energy concepts for deeper discussion.
Merged Elements: Sync‑Pulse heat‑map, Echo Bubble auto‑grouping, Practical silent removal step, minimal tech requirement.
